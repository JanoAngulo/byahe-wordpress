Galing Bulacan.

From Bulacan, for everyone. BYAHE started at a bakery counter in Malolos and now helps small businesses across the country get paid.

It started with a notebook of utang.

In 2023, our co-founder Rina was helping at her family's bakery in Malolos. Half the regulars paid in cash, a third paid "next week", and the rest sent screenshots nobody could match to an order.

She and Paolo, an engineer from the same town, built a simple QR invoice over one long weekend. Ten neighboring shops asked for it within a month. That was the first BYAHE.

Today the team works out of an office in BGC, but every feature still gets tested at a real counter in Bulacan first.

Our mission.

Every small business in the Philippines should get paid on time, in full, without chasing anyone.

From a bakery in Malolos to an office in BGC, that's the only test a new feature has to pass.

What we hold to.

  • Pesos, not jargon

    Prices, fees and reports in plain words and plain pesos. If a sari-sari owner can't read it, we rewrite it.

  • Built for the counter

    Every feature works on a cheap Android phone, on mobile data, with a line of customers waiting.

  • Ligtas muna

    Safety first. We'd rather ship a week late than ship something that puts someone's money at risk.

  • Walang iwanan

    No one gets left behind. Free plans stay free, and support answers in English or Tagalog.
