Is Libre really free?

Yes. Libre has no monthly fee and no time limit. You pay only the per-payment fee when a customer pays you, the same as on every plan.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time. Upgrades start right away. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, and you keep your invoices and receipts.

Do my customers need the BYAHE app?

No. They pay from the link or QR code with the bank or e-wallet app they already use, or with a card in their browser.

When does the money reach my bank?

QR Ph and InstaPay payments settle the same day. Card payments and PESONet settle the next banking day.

Are the receipts accepted by the BIR?

In a real product they would follow BIR receipt rules. BYAHE is a demo company, so nothing here is a valid BIR document.

Is BYAHE a real company?

No. BYAHE is a fictional company made up for a portfolio project. No accounts, no payments and no data are collected.
